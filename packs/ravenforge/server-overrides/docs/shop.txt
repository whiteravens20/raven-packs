The server shop
===============

The operator's shop block never runs out, has no owner and keeps no chest. The
payment it takes leaves the economy entirely — it is not a shop so much as an
incinerator with a counter. That is the whole point: it is the other end of the
money supply from the minting recipe, and the only thing holding RavenCoin's
value up.

An entry is N of one item for M of another. The price does not have to be coins,
and an entry can be gated on a rank. You configure each block in game by picking
the two items out of your own inventory, so this file is a list of decisions,
not a list of commands.


1. The exchange rate
--------------------

Minting is GGG / DAD / GGG — six gold ingots, two diamonds, one amethyst shard,
for one coin block, which is nine RavenCoin.

Two of those three are effectively unlimited. Gold and diamond come out of a
quarry through Mekanism's five-times ore processing and nobody counts them.

Amethyst is the one that is not, and that was audited rather than assumed:
every recipe, loot table and worldgen file in all ninety-nine mods was read,
looking for anything that outputs amethyst. What came back:

  * the compression mod's crates and compressed blocks — all forty recipes are
    exactly 9:1 and 1:9, so they store amethyst, they do not make it
  * Immersive Engineering's and Railcraft's crushers — one amethyst block into
    four shards, which is what the vanilla crafting grid already does
  * everything else that mentions amethyst takes it as an INPUT: tinted glass,
    EnderIO's fused quartz, the nucleosynthesizer's echo shards, allay
    duplication, and our own minting recipe
  * ONE REAL PRODUCER, now closed. Immersive Engineering's Excavator had an
    "amethyst crevasse" mineral vein at weight 10, in every overworld biome. At
    the pack's configured yield of 4096 that is roughly 1 475 amethyst blocks,
    or 5 900 shards, or 53 000 RavenCoin out of a single vein — a third of the
    top rank, dug up by a machine. The pack's datapack drops that vein.

Two more routes were closed before this audit and belong in the same list. The
gold and diamond laser lenses are banned from ever being sold — section 5. And
Mekanism's cardboard box is blocked from picking up budding amethyst, which
otherwise let a player carry a whole geode home for the price of four sawdust.

So the real rate, and the sentence to price everything against, is:

    ONE AMETHYST SHARD = NINE RAVENCOIN

Before quoting a price, convert it. "450 coin" says nothing. "Fifty shards of
amethyst" says everything.

The supply is throttled by something already in the pack and worth knowing
about: a claimed geode only grows while its owner is online, because forceload
only runs then (decision D17). Amethyst production is proportional to time at
the keyboard, not to time on the calendar. A geode with about twenty usable
budding blocks is worth roughly 900 RavenCoin an hour, and the same player still
has to feed the recipe 600 gold and 200 diamonds an hour to turn it into money.

One measured leak, left open deliberately: Mekanism's Antiprotonic
Nucleosynthesizer turns one coal and 4 mB of antimatter into a diamond in fifty
seconds. It has no recipe for gold and none for amethyst. Diamond was never the
binding input, the machine sits behind the deepest gate in the pack, and it is
slow — so this is recorded rather than closed. If the SPS ever gets cheaper,
revisit it.


2. Where the shop sits in a season
----------------------------------

Worth saying plainly, because it looks like a flaw until you see it whole: a
player can reach Technik without buying anything here except the rank.

That cannot be fixed, and it should not be. The road to 150 000 is limited by
amethyst, and amethyst is the currency. Nothing the shop could sell makes
amethyst grow faster. The only two items that would genuinely shorten the road
are the gold and diamond lenses, and those are banned forever for exactly that
reason.

So the shape is:

    before Technik    small conveniences, nothing more
    Technik           the one real money goal
    the finale        the shop is the only thing left to do

That last line is what makes the six-week finale work. Three players hold the
top rank, the ladder is finished, the income keeps coming and there is nowhere
for it to go but here. The brown lens at 100 000 stops being an expensive toy
and becomes the finish line.


3. What the shop sells
----------------------

Laser Drill lenses. Every one of the sixteen is banned from crafting, so this
shop is their only source in the world, which is what makes this list part of
the currency balance rather than a convenience.

A lens is NOT consumed. It is a one-off payment for an endless stream of ore,
and the prices reflect that.

The colours below are the ones that produce anything in THIS pack. The full
table in Industrial Foregoing lists ores from mods that are not here.

    lens         what it yields here                          price
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    brown        ANCIENT DEBRIS (weight 1/46), iron,         100 000
                 aluminium, nickel                           + Technik only
    lime         emerald, uranium                              8 000
    light_gray   osmium, tin, fluorite                         8 000
    red          redstone (weight 32)                          5 000
    white        nether quartz                                 2 500
    purple       lead                                          2 500
    gray         silver                                          800
    orange       copper                                          800
    blue         lapis                                           800
    black        coal                                            400

    green        nothing — peridot is not in this pack       do not list
    magenta      nothing — arcane is not in this pack        do not list

    yellow       GOLD                                        NEVER SELL
    light_blue   DIAMOND                                     NEVER SELL

Brown is not really a lens, it is a netherite tap, and it is priced with the
rank rather than with the other lenses. Behind the Technik gate the whole thing
costs 250 000 — rank plus lens — and that is the intended end of the road.

The cheap end is priced for a player who has not reached Obywatel yet. Four
hundred coin for a coal lens is not meant to burn money; it is meant to get a
new player through the door so they find out the shop exists.


4. What the shop buys
---------------------

This is the dangerous half. Every buy-back entry turns an item's crafting chain
into a money printer running at the speed of that chain — and in a pack with AE2
autocrafting, anything craftable eventually becomes infinite. The prices below
are deliberately lower than the build feels like it deserves. We are not paying
for effort; we are paying little enough that the printer stays slower than the
mint.

Two parallel ladders, one on each tech tree, so a player can earn in whichever
one they have built.

    early endgame — a fission reactor, or basic AE2
    ---------------------------------------------------------------
    QIO Hyper Dense Drive     mekanism:qio_drive_hyper_dense    1 500
    MEGA 16M Item Cell                                          1 500
    MEGA 64M Item Cell                                          6 000
    QIO Time Dilating Drive   mekanism:qio_drive_time_dilating  8 000

    deep endgame — the SPS, or full AE2
    ---------------------------------------------------------------
    MEGA Bulk Item Cell       megacells:bulk_item_cell         20 000
    MEGA 256M Item Cell       megacells:item_storage_cell_256m 25 000
    MEGA 256M Fluid Cell      megacells:fluid_storage_cell_256m 25 000
    Supermassive QIO Drive    mekanism:qio_drive_supermassive  50 000

Both ladders pay MORE THAN FOUR TIMES the rung below. That is not decoration: a
tier costs three or four of the tier under it, so if the price were merely
proportional a player would be indifferent, and would then farm the cheapest
rung because it needs no deep automation. Superlinear pricing makes crafting
upward always the better move, and that is the only thing keeping the ladder
from collapsing into spam.

Measured, so the prices are not guesses. One MEGA 256M cell is 243 AE2 256k
components and 121 accumulation processors, each of those needing its own sky
steel print, plus 16 matter balls. The item and fluid versions differ only in
the housing metal — sky steel against sky bronze, which is iron against copper —
so they cost the same and are priced the same. One Supermassive QIO Drive
expands to 256 ultimate control circuits, 256 lead, 80 plutonium, 8 polonium,
16 teleportation cores, 64 ender pearls and one antimatter pellet, and the
antimatter means an SPS.

The Bulk Item Cell needs three netherite ingots, which closes a loop with the
brown lens. It also needs ae2:spatial_cell_component_2 — note that the pack's
spatial ban covers the finished cells, the IO port, the pylon and the anchor,
but NOT the components. That is what keeps MEGA Bulk cells working, and it is
deliberate.


5. Four rules that must not break
---------------------------------

NEVER SELL THE YELLOW OR LIGHT BLUE LENS, at any price, behind any rank. They
are the gold and diamond taps, which is to say they are the mint's own inputs.
Sell one and the exchange rate in section 1 stops meaning anything.

NEVER LIST THE SAME ITEM AS BOTH A PRODUCT AND A PRICE. Buy low, sell high, in
two clicks, forever. This is the only way the shop itself can loop.

NO MACHINE MAY PRODUCE AN INPUT OF THE CURRENCY. It is the rule the three
closures in section 1 have in common, and it is the one to apply to anything
added later. A geode is effort — it only grows while its owner is online. An
excavator is capital — it runs whenever it has power. The moment a machine
makes amethyst, gold or diamond at scale, the exchange rate is set by whoever
built the largest factory rather than by anybody's time, and every price in
this file is a guess again.

NEVER BUY A RAW MACHINE OUTPUT. Buy assembled items only. A polonium pellet has
no crafting step — it is simply whatever the reactor produces, and a Mekanism
fission reactor scales with how large it is built, so the rate is set by capital
rather than by effort. Any flat price on a scaling output eventually breaks:
priced for a modest reactor, a maximal one earns ten times as much. Worse,
polonium is an ingredient in the drives above, so if raw pellets ever pay better
per pellet than the assembled drive, nobody assembles anything again. The safe
version of that idea is already in section 4: buy the drives that eat the
pellets.


6. The money supply
-------------------

The catalogue costs one fully equipped player 288 000 once — 150 000 for
Technik, 100 000 for brown, 38 000 for the rest of the lenses. Ten of them is
2.9 million of lifetime sink, and spread over a season of roughly 3 000
player-hours that needs about 960 RavenCoin an hour of faucet. A committed
player earns around 800. The two numbers meeting is the whole calibration: over
one season, a dedicated player can buy the catalogue almost exactly once.

    total supply, target        300 000 - 600 000
    median active player         15 000 -  40 000
    the richest                  up to   400 000

Three alarms:

  * under 100 000 total — deflation. Nobody can afford anything, the shop is
    scenery and players go back to barter. Raise the buy-back.
  * over 1 500 000 total — somebody found a printer. Work out which buy-back
    entry is being farmed.
  * ONE PLAYER HOLDING OVER HALF THE SUPPLY — the buy-back is automated. This
    is the single number worth watching, and the only one that means the design
    has failed rather than drifted.

None of this is measurable yet. There is no command for the total supply, and
/baltop only sees banked balances — coins sitting in a chest are invisible. Add
/rc eco total in the next release of the mod; the balance store is already
there and it is a sum over a map. Until then the proxy is the sum of the /baltop
top ten, read once a week, which will not see the chests but will show the trend
and will show anybody pulling away from the rest.
