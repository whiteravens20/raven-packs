Seasons
=======

A modded server without an ending drifts. The map fills up, three mega-bases do
everything, nobody new can find unclaimed ground, and the server does not so
much finish as go quiet. A season gives the world a shape: it starts, it is
climbed, it ends, and the next one starts clean.

The ladder in ranks.txt and the price list in shop.txt are both written for one
season. They are calibrated so that a committed player can buy the catalogue
almost exactly once — see shop.txt section 6 — and that calibration only holds
if the money and the ladder reset with the world.


1. How a season ends
--------------------

Two triggers. Whichever fires first.

    THREE PLAYERS HOLD TECHNIK
        the finale opens: six weeks on the clock, then the season ends

    TEN MONTHS HAVE PASSED
        the season ends regardless — the backstop for a quiet season

The six weeks are not a formality. Without them the third player buys the rank
one evening and the server ends the same night, for everybody, with no warning.
With them the ending is announced, the players who are close have time to also
reach Technik and earn the mark, and the last stretch is the busiest part of the
season instead of a wall.

Three, not five, because this server runs fifteen slots. Set this number against
the people actually playing: if half the regulars can never realistically get
there, the season never ends and the backstop does all the work.

Check it with a command that already exists:

    lp group technik listmembers


2. Why the finale is when the shop matters
------------------------------------------

A player can reach Technik without buying anything in the shop except the rank,
and that is not a flaw to be patched. The road to 150 000 is limited by
amethyst, amethyst is the currency, and nothing the shop could sell makes
amethyst grow faster.

So the shop's real job starts at the top of the ladder, not below it. During the
finale three players hold Technik, the ladder is finished, income keeps arriving
and there is nowhere for it to go. The brown lens at 100 000 stops being an
expensive toy and becomes the finish line — at the rates in shop.txt, six weeks
is enough to afford it if you push, and not if you do not.


3. What carries over
--------------------

The world, the ladder, the claims and the balances all go. What survives is a
mark.

Each finished season leaves a LuckPerms group with no powers at all — no
permissions, no claim allowance, nothing but a suffix. Everybody who held
Technik when the season ended is put in it, and it follows them into every
season after.

One detail that is easy to get backwards. LuckPerms shows one suffix, the one
with the highest priority. Give the priority to the OLDEST season, not the
newest. A player who was here in the first season and is still playing in the
fourth should show [S1], because "I was here at the start" is the thing worth
displaying; if the newest wins, that player is indistinguishable from someone
who arrived last week. The full list is in `lp user <name> info` either way.

An optional second grade, if you want the finale to reward spending rather than
only earning: a stronger mark for anyone who also bought the brown lens before
the season closed. Then the last achievement of a season is a purchase, not a
balance — which is the right way round, because a competition for who ends up
richest is a competition for who used the shop least.


4. Closing a season
-------------------

In order. Some of these cannot be undone, so do them in this one.

    1.  Announce the finale when the third Technik is bought. Six weeks.
    2.  At the end: `lp group technik listmembers`, and put every one of them
        into the new season group.
    3.  Take a backup of the whole server directory. Not a world save — the
        directory, including config/luckperms/.
    4.  Wipe world/. Remember that world/datapacks/ lives inside it: keep a copy
        of ravenforge-mining and ravenforge-blocks, or restore them from the
        pack, or the mining dimension comes back missing.
    5.  Reset the ladder: every player back to `nowy`, balances to zero. The
        season groups stay.
    6.  New world. Then docs/world.txt from the top — set spawn, set the border,
        pregenerate. Pregeneration has to be redone; it is per world.
    7.  Server claim and the warp on the mining platform, as in the mine notes.


5. Numbers to watch during a season
-----------------------------------

    lp group technik listmembers      how close the finale is
    /rc eco total                     the supply, and who is pulling away

The second one matters more than it looks. A single player holding more than
half the money in the world does not mean they are good at the game; it means a
buy-back entry is being automated, and shop.txt section 6 says what to do about
it. /rc eco total says that share outright and warns when it passes half. Look
at it weekly, not at the end.

Do not rank the season on /baltop. It sees banked balances only, and with the
bank at spawn a player can finish the season rich and absent from it. The
per-player figure that counts is /rc eco worth <player>.
